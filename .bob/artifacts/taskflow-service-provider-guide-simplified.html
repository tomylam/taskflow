Service Provider User Guide

Getting Started

Create Your Account

  1. Click "Register" and select "Service Provider"
  2. Enter your email and password
  3. Verify your email address
  4. Complete your profile with skills and availability

Task Lifecycle

1
PENDING_QUOTE

Task is open for bids

2
QUOTED

You submitted a quote - waiting for acceptance

3
IN_PROGRESS

Quote accepted - start working

4
SUBMITTED

Work uploaded for review

5
COMPLETED

Work approved

Finding & Bidding on Tasks

Browse Available Tasks

  • Go to "Available Tasks" to see all open tasks
  • Click on any task to view details and requirements
  • Review attached files and materials

Submit a Quote

  1. Open a task you want to bid on
  2. Click "Submit Quote"
  3. Enter your price and delivery date
  4. Add optional notes about your approach
  5. Click "Submit"

Working on Your Task

After Quote Accepted

  • Task moves to IN_PROGRESS
  • You can now upload files and communicate with the distributor
  • Use the chat to ask questions or provide updates

Upload Your Work

  1. Go to the task detail page
  2. Click "Upload Files" or drag and drop
  3. Add a message explaining your progress
  4. Files are stored securely in cloud storage

Submitting Your Work

  1. Upload your final files
  2. Send a message confirming work is ready
  3. Change task status to "SUBMITTED"
  4. Wait for distributor review and feedback

If Revisions Requested

  • Read feedback in the chat
  • Ask for clarification if needed
  • Make requested changes
  • Upload updated files
  • Send message when done

Communication

  • Use in-task chat for all communication
  • Respond to messages within 24 hours
  • Ask questions early if requirements are unclear
  • Send progress updates regularly
  • Be professional and honest in all messages

Your Dashboard

Your dashboard shows:

  • Active tasks you're working on
  • Pending quotes awaiting decisions
  • Available tasks to bid on
  • Completed tasks history
  • Your rating and completion rate

Account Settings

  • Update email and password
  • Manage your skills and specializations
  • Set availability and working hours
  • Configure notification preferences

FAQ

How long does review take?

Most distributors review within 24-48 hours.

Can I submit multiple quotes per task?

No, only one quote per task. Make it count.

What if I miss the deadline?

Contact the distributor immediately via chat to discuss options.

Are files secure?

Yes, all files are encrypted and only accessible to you and the distributor.

How do I increase my rating?

Deliver quality work on time and communicate professionally.
