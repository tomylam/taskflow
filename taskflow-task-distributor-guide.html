Task Distributor User Guide

Welcome to TaskFlow

TaskFlow is a cloud-based platform that connects you with qualified service providers who can help complete your academic assignments. This guide will help you post tasks, manage quotes, review work, and ensure quality deliverables.

📌 Getting Started: Register your account, verify your email, and set up your profile. Then you're ready to post your first task and connect with service providers.

Account Setup & Registration

Creating Your Account

Step 1: Visit the TaskFlow login page and click "Don't have an account? Register"
Step 2: Fill in your details:
  • Email: Use a valid email address you check regularly
  • Password: Create a strong password (8+ characters, mix of letters, numbers, symbols)
  • Account Type: Select "Work Distributor"
Step 3: Click "Register" and verify your email address

Completing Your Profile

A complete profile builds trust with service providers:

  • Full Name: Your real name or organization name
  • Profile Photo: Professional photo or organization logo
  • Bio/Description: Brief background about yourself or your organization
  • Contact Information: Secondary email or phone for important matters
  • Preferred Subjects: Areas where you typically need help (optional)

Understanding the Task Lifecycle

Every task follows a structured workflow. As a distributor, you manage it through each stage:

1
PENDING_QUOTE

You've posted the task. Service providers are submitting quotes. Review incoming bids and choose the best one.

2
QUOTED

A service provider has submitted a quote. You accept or reject it. Accepting moves the task to IN_PROGRESS.

3
IN_PROGRESS

The provider is working on your task. Communicate, review drafts, and monitor progress.

4
SUBMITTED

Provider has uploaded the completed work. Review it carefully and decide: approve or request revisions.

5
COMPLETED

You've approved the work. Task is complete, payment is processed, and you can leave a rating.

⚡
REVISION (any time)

You can request revisions while in IN_PROGRESS or SUBMITTED stages. Provider goes back to IN_PROGRESS to make changes.

Posting Your First Task

Getting Started

Click "Create New Task" or "Post a Task" from your dashboard to begin:

Step 1: Click "Create Task"
Step 2: Fill in the task details (see sections below)
Step 3: Upload any reference files or materials
Step 4: Set your budget and deadline
Step 5: Review and click "Post Task"

Task Title & Description

Title: Be clear and specific. Examples:

  • ❌ Bad: "Essay needed"
  • ✅ Good: "1500-word essay on climate change policy (APA format, due Dec 15)"

Description: Include:

  • Assignment requirements and objectives
  • Word count or page length
  • Format (APA, MLA, Chicago, etc.)
  • Specific topics or sections to cover
  • Any rubrics, grading criteria, or expectations
  • Subject matter or course context
  • Tone and style preferences

Task Category & Subject

Select the most relevant category to help service providers find your task:

  • Essay Writing
  • Research & Analysis
  • Mathematics & Statistics
  • Programming & Code
  • Data Analysis
  • Lab Reports
  • Case Studies
  • Other

Uploading Reference Materials

Attach files that help service providers understand your requirements:

  • Assignment PDF: The original assignment document
  • Rubric: Grading criteria if available
  • Sample Work: Examples of the quality/style you expect
  • Research Materials: Articles, sources, or reference documents
  • Template: Any specific format or structure to follow

📁 Supported Formats: PDF, Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), Images, and more. No file size limits.

Setting Budget & Deadline

Field What to Do Why It Matters
Budget Set a realistic price based on task complexity and timeline Affects quote quality; too low may attract inexperienced providers
Deadline Set a deadline at least 2-3 days before your actual due date Gives you time to review and request revisions if needed
Visibility Choose between Public (all providers) or Invite-Only Public gets more quotes; Invite-Only lets you contact specific providers

AI-Powered Task Analysis

TaskFlow automatically analyzes your task using AI (Google Gemini) to:

  • Identify key requirements and deliverables
  • Suggest estimated complexity level
  • Highlight important details service providers should know
  • Flag potential ambiguities or missing information

Review the AI analysis and adjust your description if needed to make requirements crystal clear.

Managing Quotes & Selecting a Service Provider

Receiving Quotes

After posting your task, service providers will submit quotes. You'll see:

  • Provider's name, profile photo, and rating
  • Proposed price and delivery date
  • Optional cover message explaining their approach
  • Provider's specializations and past completion rate

Evaluating Quotes

Consider these factors when choosing a provider:

Price

Competitive pricing without being suspiciously cheap. Don't just pick the lowest bid.

Rating

Look for providers with 4.5+ stars and positive reviews. New providers may have no history.

Specialization

Choose someone with expertise in your subject. Check their previous work and reviews.

Responsiveness

A good cover message and quick quote submission suggest they're engaged and professional.

Delivery Timeline

Ensure they can deliver in time. Realistic timelines are better than rushed promises.

Communication

Did they ask clarifying questions? This shows they carefully read your task.

Accepting or Rejecting Quotes

To Accept: Click "Accept Quote" on the provider's bid. The task moves to IN_PROGRESS and you begin working together.
To Reject: Click "Reject" and optionally provide feedback to help the provider improve. Other providers can still bid.

💡 Pro Tip: Once you find a great provider, you can contact them directly for future tasks. Build long-term relationships!

Managing Multiple Quotes

You can review multiple quotes before deciding. Best practices:

  • Don't accept the first quote unless it's clearly the best
  • Wait 24-48 hours to give providers time to submit
  • Compare at least 3-5 quotes before making a decision
  • Look for quality indicators (rating, experience, detailed cover messages)
  • Trust your gut—if something feels off, it probably is

Working with Service Providers

Communication & Messaging

Once a provider is selected, you have a built-in chat for each task:

  • Ask clarifying questions or provide additional guidance
  • Request progress updates at key milestones
  • Share feedback on drafts or intermediate submissions
  • Discuss any changes or revised requirements
  • Keep a record of all communications in one place
💡 Communication Best Practices: Be clear, specific, and professional. Provide feedback promptly. If something is wrong, explain it kindly—providers want to get it right.

Monitoring Task Progress

Track progress through the task detail page:

  • Stage Updates: Provider updates their progress status
  • File Uploads: View drafts and intermediate work
  • Messages: See all communications in the chat thread
  • Timeline: Visual progress tracking from start to completion

Requesting Revisions

If the work doesn't meet your expectations:

Step 1: Clearly explain what needs to be changed in the chat
Step 2: Provide specific feedback with examples if possible
Step 3: Move the task back to "IN_PROGRESS" or "REVISION" status
Step 4: The provider makes changes and resubmits

⚠️ Important: Be reasonable with revision requests. The provider accepted your terms for a specific price. Major scope changes may warrant renegotiation.

Handling Issues & Disputes

Issue How to Handle
Provider is unresponsive Wait 24 hours, then send a follow-up message. If no response after 48 hours, contact support.
Work quality is poor Request revisions with specific feedback. If provider can't meet standards, reject and contact support.
Deadline is at risk Communicate urgently via chat. Ask for status updates. Contact alternative providers if needed.
Plagiarism suspected Request original work. Use plagiarism detection tools. Contact support with evidence.
Scope creep Clarify in chat what's included in the original quote. Negotiate additional payment if needed.

Reviewing & Approving Work

When Work is Submitted

The provider will upload their final deliverable and mark the task as "SUBMITTED". You'll receive a notification to review.

Step 1: Download and thoroughly review the work
Step 2: Check against requirements: formatting, content quality, deadline compliance
Step 3: Use plagiarism detection if concerned (Turnitin, Grammarly, etc.)
Step 4: Decide: Approve or request revisions

Quality Checklist

  • ✓ Meets all requirements listed in the assignment
  • ✓ Proper formatting (font, spacing, margins, references)
  • ✓ Correct citation style (APA, MLA, Chicago, etc.)
  • ✓ No grammatical errors or typos
  • ✓ Appropriate length (word count, pages)
  • ✓ Addresses all rubric points if provided
  • ✓ Original work (not plagiarized)
  • ✓ Submitted on time

Approving the Work

If the work meets your standards:

  • Click "Approve" or "Mark as Complete"
  • Leave a positive rating and review (1-5 stars)
  • Share specific praise in your feedback
  • Payment is processed automatically
  • Task moves to "COMPLETED" status

Leaving a Rating & Review

Help build the TaskFlow community by providing honest feedback:

  • 5 Stars: Excellent work, professional communication, on-time delivery
  • 4 Stars: Good work with minor issues; still meets expectations
  • 3 Stars: Acceptable work but with some problems; required revisions
  • 2 Stars: Poor quality; significant issues despite revisions
  • 1 Star: Unacceptable work; did not meet requirements at all

Write a brief review (optional but helpful) highlighting strengths and areas for improvement.

✓ Set Realistic Budgets

Research typical rates for your task type. Paying too little attracts inexperienced providers. Paying fairly attracts quality work.

✓ Build Long-Term Relationships

After finding a great provider, contact them directly for future tasks. Repeat customers often offer better rates and quality.

✓ Communicate Proactively

Don't wait for updates. Check in periodically. Provide feedback on drafts. Answer provider questions quickly.

✓ Plan Ahead

Post tasks at least 3-5 days before your actual deadline. This gives providers time to bid and you time to review and request revisions.

Detailed, constructive feedback helps the platform and future distributors. Be fair—acknowledge good work and specific issues.

Always check for plagiarism, formatting, and accuracy before approving. Use plagiarism detection tools if available. Your grade is at stake.

Request revisions if work doesn't meet specs. Don't approve substandard work just to move on. Providers should meet the agreed-upon quality.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How quickly will I receive quotes after posting a task?
A: It depends on demand and timing. You may receive quotes within minutes, or it may take hours. Quality quotes often come within the first 24 hours. Wait at least 24 hours before accepting to give more providers a chance to bid.
Q: Can I change the budget or deadline after posting a task?
A: This depends on your platform settings. It's generally recommended not to change terms after posting, as it may confuse providers. Contact support if you need to modify a task.
Q: What if I'm not satisfied with a provider's work?
A: Request revisions with specific feedback. If the provider still can't meet your standards, you can reject the work and contact another provider. Be fair—explain exactly what's wrong.
Q: Can I accept a quote and then cancel later?
A: Yes, but it's not recommended. Canceling after acceptance may harm your reputation and the provider's ability to work. If you must cancel, communicate immediately with a good reason and consider partial payment as compensation.
Q: How do I prevent plagiarism?
A: Always check submitted work with plagiarism detection software (Turnitin, Grammarly, etc.). Ask providers for original work only. You can request proof of originality if suspicious. Never approve work without verification.
Q: Can I contact a provider directly outside of TaskFlow?
A: It's better to keep all communication within TaskFlow for your protection and record-keeping. However, you can exchange contact info privately after the task is completed if both parties agree.
Q: What happens if a provider doesn't meet the deadline?
A: Check in via chat immediately. If they're close to finishing, give them a small extension. If they abandon the task, reject their work and hire someone else. Document everything for support if needed.
Q: How are my files stored and protected?
A: All files are stored securely in Google Drive. They're encrypted and only accessible to you and the assigned provider. Files are deleted when the task is completed or after 30 days of inactivity, per your privacy settings.
Q: Can I reuse a provider for multiple tasks?
A: Absolutely! If you had a great experience, contact them directly or mention them in future task postings. Many providers prefer working with returning customers and may offer better rates for loyalty.
Q: What if there's a dispute or disagreement over payment?
A: Contact TaskFlow support with evidence of the issue. Our team will review the task, messages, and work submitted to help resolve the dispute fairly.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

⚠️ Mistake #1: Vague Task Descriptions

Posting "I need an essay" without details leads to poor quotes and work. Be specific about requirements, format, length, and deadlines.

⚠️ Mistake #2: Choosing Only by Price

The cheapest quote isn't always the best. Quality matters more than cost. A $5 essay that fails is worse than a $30 essay that gets an A.

⚠️ Mistake #3: Setting Impossible Deadlines

Expecting a 5000-word research paper in 12 hours is unrealistic. Good work takes time. Set deadlines at least 3 days before your actual due date.

⚠️ Mistake #4: Poor Communication

Not responding to provider questions or updates breeds confusion and poor results. Check messages daily and respond promptly.

⚠️ Mistake #5: Approving Without Review

Always verify work quality, plagiarism, and adherence to requirements before approving. Don't approve just to finish—your grade depends on it.

⚠️ Mistake #6: Scope Creep

Don't keep asking for "one more thing" or major revisions after the provider completed the original scope. This damages relationships and may warrant additional payment.

⚠️ Mistake #7: Ignoring Plagiarism Concerns

If work seems suspicious or too polished, verify it. Use plagiarism detection. Never submit potentially plagiarized work as your own.
