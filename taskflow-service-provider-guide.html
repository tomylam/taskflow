Service Provider User Guide

Welcome to TaskFlow

TaskFlow is a cloud-based platform that connects academic work distributors with service providers like you. This guide will help you understand how to find tasks, submit quotes, complete work, and communicate with distributors.

📌 Getting Started: To begin, register for an account, verify your email, and complete your profile. You'll then be able to browse available tasks and submit quotes.

Account Setup & Registration

Creating Your Account

Step 1: Visit the TaskFlow login page and click "Don't have an account? Register"
Step 2: Fill in your details:
  • Email: Use a professional email address
  • Password: Create a strong password (8+ characters, mix of letters, numbers, symbols)
  • Account Type: Select "Service Provider"
Step 3: Click "Register" and verify your email address

Completing Your Profile

Once registered, log in and complete your profile to increase your chances of winning tasks:

  • Professional Photo: Upload a clear profile picture
  • Bio/Description: Highlight your expertise and experience
  • Specializations: Select your areas of expertise (e.g., Essay Writing, Research, Mathematics, etc.)
  • Availability: Set your working hours and response time

Understanding the Task Lifecycle

Every task moves through defined stages. Here's how it works:

1
PENDING_QUOTE

Distributor has posted a task and is waiting for service providers to submit quotes

2
QUOTED

You've submitted a quote. Distributor reviews and decides to accept or reject it

3
IN_PROGRESS

Your quote was accepted! Begin working on the task and submit updates

4
SUBMITTED

You've uploaded your completed work for the distributor to review

5
COMPLETED

Distributor approved your work. Task is complete and you receive payment

⚡
REVISION (any time)

Distributor requests changes. Move back to IN_PROGRESS to make revisions

Finding & Bidding on Tasks

Browsing Available Tasks

Navigate to the "Available Tasks" or "Dashboard" section to see all open tasks:

  • Tasks are displayed as cards showing task title, description, deadline, and budget
  • Click on any task to view full details, requirements, and attached files
  • Filter by category, deadline, or budget range to find tasks that match your skills
  • Read the task description carefully—it may include rubrics, requirements, or samples

Submitting a Quote

When you find a task you want to bid on:

Step 1: Open the task detail page
Step 2: Click the "Submit Quote" button
Step 3: Enter your proposed price and delivery timeline
Step 4: (Optional) Add a cover message explaining why you're the right fit
Step 5: Click "Submit Quote"

💡 Pro Tip: Be realistic with pricing and timelines. Distributors are more likely to accept quotes that are competitive yet professional.

Quote Status Tracking

Status Meaning What You Can Do
PENDING Distributor hasn't reviewed yet Wait or send a message asking about timeline
ACCEPTED Distributor approved your quote! Start working. Task moves to IN_PROGRESS
REJECTED Distributor chose another provider View feedback if provided; submit new quote on similar tasks

Working on Your Tasks

After Your Quote is Accepted

Once a distributor accepts your quote, the task moves to IN_PROGRESS:

  • You'll see the full task details, deadline, and any attachments
  • The task will appear in your "Active Tasks" list
  • You can now upload drafts, ask questions via chat, and track progress
  • Update task stages to show progress (e.g., "Research Started", "First Draft Ready", etc.)

Uploading Files & Progress Updates

To share your work and keep the distributor informed:

Step 1: Open the task and scroll to the "Files" section
Step 2: Click "Upload File" or drag-and-drop your work
Step 3: Files are securely stored in Google Drive
Step 4: Add progress updates via the chat to explain what you've done

📁 Supported Formats: PDF, Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), Images (.png, .jpg), Videos, and more. No file size limits.

Task Stages & Status Updates

Update your progress through predefined stages:

  • PENDING: You've started on the task
  • IN_PROGRESS: You're actively working
  • REVIEW_READY: First draft is ready for distributor feedback
  • REVISION: Distributor requested changes
  • SUBMITTED: Final work is ready for acceptance
💡 Communication Tip: Regularly update the task status and send messages. This builds trust and shows you're actively engaged.

Communication & Messaging

Chat with Distributors

Every task has a built-in messaging system for real-time communication:

  • Click the "Messages" or "Chat" tab on any task detail page
  • Send questions, clarifications, or status updates instantly
  • Receive notifications when the distributor replies
  • Keep all conversations organized within the task thread

Best Practices for Communication

  • Be Professional: Use clear, grammatically correct English
  • Be Responsive: Reply within 24 hours when possible
  • Ask Clarifying Questions: If the requirements are unclear, ask early
  • Provide Regular Updates: Send progress updates even if not explicitly requested
  • Be Honest: If you'll miss a deadline, communicate immediately
  • Document Changes: If the distributor requests revisions, confirm via chat what you'll change

Submitting & Finalizing Work

When You're Ready to Submit

Step 1: Upload your final deliverable to the "Files" section
Step 2: Send a message confirming your work is complete and ready for review
Step 3: Update the task status to "SUBMITTED"
Step 4: Wait for the distributor to review and provide feedback

Handling Revisions

If the distributor requests changes:

  • Carefully read their feedback and questions in the chat
  • Clarify any ambiguous feedback by asking follow-up questions
  • Make the requested revisions and upload the updated file
  • Update the task status to "IN_PROGRESS" or "REVISION" to show you're working
  • Send a message confirming the revisions are complete
  • Repeat until the distributor is satisfied and approves the work

Task Completion & Payment

Once the distributor marks the task as "COMPLETED":

  • Your work has been approved and accepted
  • Payment is processed according to the agreed rate
  • The task moves to your "Completed" history
  • You may receive a rating or review from the distributor

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How long does it take for a distributor to review my quote?
A: This varies. Most distributors review within 24-48 hours, but some may take longer. If you haven't heard back after 3 days, feel free to send a polite message asking about the timeline.
Q: Can I submit multiple quotes for the same task?
A: No. You can only submit one quote per task. If your quote is rejected, you can submit a new quote at a later time. Be strategic and make your first quote count.
Q: What if I can't complete a task before the deadline?
A: Contact the distributor immediately via chat. Explain the situation and propose an extension. Be honest—distributors appreciate transparency. If you can't complete it at all, it's better to communicate early than to disappear.
Q: Can I cancel a quote I submitted?
A: Once submitted, you cannot directly cancel a quote. However, if the distributor accepts it and you truly cannot do the work, contact them immediately to discuss your options. Canceling after acceptance may negatively impact your rating.
Q: How are files stored and are they secure?
A: All files are stored securely in Google Drive. They're encrypted and only accessible to you and the distributor working on that task. Files are not shared with other users.
Q: What happens if I disagree with a task status or rating?
A: Contact the distributor through the chat to discuss your concerns. If you cannot resolve it, reach out to TaskFlow support with details of the dispute. Our team will review and help mediate.
Q: How do I increase my chances of winning more tasks?
A: Maintain a strong profile with clear specializations, submit competitive and realistic quotes, deliver high-quality work on time, communicate professionally, and aim for positive distributor ratings. Your reputation is your best marketing tool.
Q: Can I work with the same distributor repeatedly?
A: Yes! If a distributor is satisfied with your work, they may post more tasks and potentially prefer to work with you again. Many service providers build long-term relationships with regular distributors.

Tips for Success

✓ Build a Strong Profile

A complete, professional profile with clear specializations and good photos increases your chances of winning tasks.

✓ Be Competitive But Professional

Price your work fairly. Underpricing damages your reputation; overpricing loses you bids. Find the sweet spot.

✓ Deliver Quality Work Early

Submit ahead of deadlines when possible. Distributors reward reliability and will post future tasks specifically for you.

✓ Communicate Proactively

Don't wait for distributors to ask questions. Update them regularly, ask clarifying questions early, and report any issues immediately.

✓ Learn from Feedback

If you receive negative feedback, take it constructively. Ask the distributor what you could improve and apply those lessons to future work.

✓ Stay Professional

Use professional language, meet commitments, and respect deadlines. Your reputation is everything in this platform.
